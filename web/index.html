❔How to use the trainer ✕
What this is

A flight simulator for NAS100 trading. Each round freezes a real chart at a decision moment between and New York time. You decide Long, Short or Skip, place entry, stop and target, then the next candles replay and you are graded. The real date is hidden so you cannot remember what happened.

About of rounds are no-trade rounds on purpose: Skip is a real answer and is scored. The trainer looks for big moves: a good trade aims for at least R.

A round, step by step
  1. Start. Pick a drill (one setup type only, or all) and the number of rounds (default ), then Enter.
  2. Read the chart. The last candle on the right is "now". Switch M5 / M15 / H1 with 1 2 3. Dashed lines are the Asian, London and previous-day highs/lows.
  3. Decide. L long, S short, K skip.
  4. Plan the order. Entry, SL and TP lines appear (SL 1×ATR, TP at R). Drag them on the chart or type prices. Watch R:R and risk in the panel.
  5. Tag it. Setup type (T), bias with/against the H1 trend (B), confidence 1–5 (Shift+1–5), and an optional note (/). Write why before you see the result.
  6. Commit with Enter. Inputs lock; no changes after this.
  7. Replay. Candles play forward. Speed: Z 1×, X 5×, C instant.
  8. Result. Your R, what happened and why, the setup type revealed, and the ideal trade (I shows it on the chart). Enter for the next round.
  9. Report. After the last round: your stats and the "work on this" list.
Placing the order
  • Market order (default, M): fills at the frozen close + points spread.
  • Pending order: drag the entry line (or type a price). SL and TP move with it, so your risk and R:R stay the same. It only fills if price reaches it before ET; otherwise "not filled".
  • Drag SL or TP on their own to change risk or target. Long: SL below entry, TP above. Short: the opposite.
  • 1R = your stop distance. A trade that hits TP at 3× the stop distance = +3R.
How you are graded
  • Trades are resolved on 1-minute candles. If one candle touches both SL and TP, it counts as a loss.
  • Trades still open at ET are closed at that price (time-out).
  • Skip is correct when neither direction reached +R before −1R (stop = ×ATR). Otherwise it is a "missed opportunity".
  • Plan quality, separate from the result: SL beyond the nearest swing, R:R ≥ , direction with the H1 trend. A good plan that loses is still a good plan.
Report, progress and coaching
  • The session report shows win rate, expectancy (average R per trade), total R, skip accuracy and plan quality, split by setup type, long/short and confidence. Tab switches to all-time.
  • "Work on this" picks your 3 costliest habits from rules. Verdicts need at least trades; below that it says "not enough data".
  • All-time progress: P on the start screen.
  • Export for review (E on the report) writes /session_<id>.md: your trades plus the chart context of every round. Attach it in a Claude chat or Claude Code; the coaching prompt is at the top of the file.

How to practise
  • 3 sessions of 10 rounds per week.
  • Judge the decision, not the outcome: read the "why" and the plan checks, not just the R.
  • After about 100 trades, keep only the setups with positive expectancy and drill them (pick the setup in "Drill" on the start screen).
